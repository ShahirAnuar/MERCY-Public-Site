# Community initiative implementation — 6 November 2026

Current owner policy supersedes the retired public, KKM/pensioner and UiTM affiliate offers. Only privately verified orphans, orphanages and OKU participants may receive free programme access and sell.

- Orphans/orphanages: For The Ones Left Behind (Bagi Yang Ditinggalkan).
- OKU: For Those In Need of a Helping Hand (Bagi Yang Memerlukan).
- Eligible attributed app sales: up to RM15 discount and 100% of actual net profit to the participant.
- Direct website app sales: 50% of verified net profit reserved for orphanages, rounded up to the next sen. A reserve is not a completed donation.
- Standard fee illustration: 3% + RM1; actual Stripe costs and confirmed applicable tax determine net profit.
- Current connected merchant payout setting: five business days, daily schedule. Merchant settlement and later publisher transfers to participants remain distinct.

The public pages include private proof applications, guardian/institution checks, participant sales/fee/payment reports and owner review of verified costs. Existing paid reader access and historical earnings remain intact. Retired account referrals and coupons cannot be advertised through the new website checkout resolver.

Validation: 103 public-page checks and 53 backend suites passed before publication. Backend version 58 is deployed from source 37309831a3643b0766f08f0aafa996368a53e26e. The live UiTM student payment link was deactivated through Stripe. Five old affiliate-only free grants were withdrawn; provider-level retirement of old promotions is being verified separately.
