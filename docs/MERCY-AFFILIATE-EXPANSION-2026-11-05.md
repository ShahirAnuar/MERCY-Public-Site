## Phrase selection

Prioritize “affiliate buku malaysia”, “cara menjadi affiliate buku”, “cara daftar affiliate”, “program affiliate buku”, “daftar affiliate buku”, “affiliate bahan bacaan keluarga malaysia” and “daftar affiliate MERCY The Guide” in useful explanations and navigation. Use “affiliate surirumah”, student, freelancer and self-employed phrases in short audience sections that explain the same actual requirements.

“Kerja affiliate” has broad employment-like intent. Explain that MERCY pays commissions on eligible verified app sales and that the amount depends on the sale and applicable conditions. Do not advertise a salary, guaranteed daily/monthly income, payment for merely registering or payment for recruiting another affiliate.

Searches also returned TikTok/Shopee brands, specific competing businesses, bank-account queries and fast-wealth titles. These are omitted because they do not describe MERCY's own programme. Do not imply MERCY app commissions are offered by TikTok, Shopee, Amazon or ClickBank.
