A curious reader’s toolkit

Four moves for deeper thinking

1. Notice

Point to a detail in the story before jumping to a conclusion.

2. Wonder

Write a question that begins with “How might…?” or “What would we need to know?”

3. Check

Separate story events, scriptural wording, evidence, and your interpretation.

4. Connect

Use two subjects to examine one idea. Notice where their questions differ.

Speak, write, draw, listen, or pass. No personal story is required. Curiosity should feel welcoming and safe.
