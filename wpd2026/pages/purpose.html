The reason behind the work

Pharmacy is more than a counter.

It is a chain of careful decisions, patient conversations, quiet checks, and people who keep showing up for the safety of others.

“

Information should feel easier to reach.

VI Awwab was created for World Pharmacists Day 2026 by the Pharmacy Department of HTAR Klang to make the role of Pharmacy more visible, understandable, and human.

01

Improve awareness

Help patients recognise how Pharmacy supports care before, during, and after a hospital visit.

02

Make safer questions possible

Give people a starting point for medication use, side effects, storage, interactions, and missed doses.

03

Honour the quiet work

Celebrate pharmacists and Pharmacy teams whose careful work is often felt by patients before it is seen.

A clear boundary

Awwab can guide. A pharmacist must assess.

This website offers general education and signposting. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a personal conversation with a pharmacist, doctor, emergency service, or poison and medication safety service.
