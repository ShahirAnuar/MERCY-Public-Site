Why Shahir Anuar Shahrani Is Described as a Polymath

The description refers to breadth across documented areas of work. It is an editorial description, not a regulated title or third-party certification. These 14 areas include professional pharmacy, invention, digital health, writing, inclusion and public policy.

This field map distinguishes completed work and roles from proposals, invitations and user-reach records. A proposal is not described as government-adopted; an invitation alone does not prove an event was delivered; app reach is not a clinical outcome.

01

Pharmacy and pharmaceutical care

Professional work as a pharmacist in Malaysia’s public health system.

02

Clinical medication safety

Warfarin Dosing HTK, a clinical dose-support application. Supplied Google Play Console records show app reach across 100+ countries and territories; this is app acquisition reach, not patients treated or clinical outcomes.

03

Pharmaceutical invention

Co-inventor named on Malaysian patent MY197771A for a tablet grinding and mixing device.

04
