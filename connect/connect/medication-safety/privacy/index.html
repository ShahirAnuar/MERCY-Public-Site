Medication Safety Game Master
Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. ABOUT THIS POLICY

Medication Safety Game Master uses Google Sign-In to provide secure participant authentication and to support saved learning progress and study-related assessment records.

2. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

When a participant signs in using Google, the application may receive basic account information provided through Google authentication, such as:

  • Google account identifier
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Basic profile information required for authentication

The application may also store information generated through use of Medication Safety Game Master, including:

  • Selected healthcare profession
  • Learning progress
  • Scenario completion
  • Assessment scores
  • Educational results
  • Relevant timestamps

3. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate participants
  • Maintain saved learning progress
  • Associate learning results with the correct participant
  • Provide educational feedback
  • Support pre- and post-learning assessment
  • Evaluate medication-safety learning outcomes
Google account information is not used to access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts or unrelated Google services.

4. STORAGE AND SECURITY

Authentication is provided through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.

Application records may be stored using Google Firebase services with access controls intended to protect participant information and restrict access appropriately.

5. SHARING OF INFORMATION

Participant personal information is not sold.

Information may be analysed for educational, quality-improvement or approved research purposes according to applicable institutional, ethical, study and data-protection requirements.

Where results are reported or published, identifying information should be removed or appropriately protected.

6. GOOGLE USER DATA

Google user data is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

The application requests only the Google account information necessary for authentication and operation of the application.

The application does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar data or Google Contacts.

7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Participant information is retained only for as long as required for legitimate educational, administrative, research, institutional or legal purposes.

Participants may contact the project administrator regarding questions about their information or requests concerning their account data, subject to applicable institutional, research and legal retention requirements.

8. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application or its data practices change.

The latest version will remain publicly available on this page.

9. CONTACT

For privacy or application-related questions, please use the existing contact information provided through the MERCY Series website.
