ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

Medication Safety Game Master is an interactive educational and assessment platform designed to strengthen safe medication practices among healthcare professionals.

The programme uses structured clinical scenarios covering the medication-use process, including prescribing, dispensing, administration, monitoring, communication, error prevention and systems-based learning.

Participants progress through seven learning topics and 28 clinical scenarios. Their responses are scored using predefined clinical decision rules, followed by educational feedback and reflective learning.

GOOGLE SIGN-IN

Google Sign-In is used to identify participants securely, save learning progress, and associate assessment results with the correct participant account.

The application does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other unrelated Google services.

RESEARCH AND LEARNING

Participant profession and learning results may be used to evaluate educational outcomes and identify areas where medication-safety knowledge can be strengthened.

Results are intended to be handled in accordance with applicable study, institutional, ethical and data-protection requirements.

EDUCATIONAL DISCLAIMER

Medication Safety Game Master is an educational simulation. It does not replace professional clinical judgement, institutional procedures, medication references or patient-specific clinical assessment.
