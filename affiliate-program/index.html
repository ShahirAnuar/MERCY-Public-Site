Affiliate questions

Can I sell without offering a discount?

Yes. Approved affiliates may share their tracking link at the full RM15 price and earn RM8 per qualifying sale before adjustments.

Are there limits on commissions?

No per-affiliate sales cap is planned. Each genuine eligible purchase is tracked independently, with anti-fraud checks and refund adjustments.

Are coupons the app's serial numbers?

No. Affiliate identifiers and coupons are for promotion and commission attribution. Purchase activation and the two-device Google account binding remain separate and private.
