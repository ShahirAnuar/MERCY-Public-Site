Transparent earnings

Your sale. Your clean profit.

For an eligible participant sale, clean profit is the actual amount received less actual payment-processing fees and confirmed applicable tax, with refunds or disputes deducted or reversed. The approved participant receives 100% of that clean profit. Publisher share: RM0.

Bagi jualan peserta yang layak, peserta menerima 100% keuntungan bersih selepas fi pembayaran sebenar, cukai berkenaan yang disahkan, serta pelarasan bayaran balik atau pertikaian. Bahagian penerbit ialah RM0.

Buyer pays RM25.00 · Estimated Stripe fee RM1.75 · Participant receives RM23.25 before applicable tax · Publisher RM0.00

Illustration only: Stripe Malaysia’s standard domestic-card processing rate is 3% + RM1.00, not 0.3% + RM1. Actual fees can vary by payment method, card country, conversion or account pricing. The dashboard uses verified transaction amounts for final earnings. Stripe Malaysia pricing.

Only active, account-owned issued codes may be shared. Choose either no discount or one issued coupon for RM1–RM15 off. Coupons cannot be stacked.

Know where your money is.

1 · Customer paysA confirmed sale first appears with its payment and fee status. A free app claim does not create sales earnings.
2 · Stripe settles the fundsStripe pays the MERCY merchant account according to its settlement and bank payout schedule. The merchant account is currently configured for a five-business-day settlement delay and daily payouts. First payouts can take 7–14 days; the actual account and payment method determine availability. Weekends, holidays, verification or bank processing can add time.
3 · MERCY transfers to youAfter reconciliation and funds availability, MERCY arranges the participant’s bank transfer. The dashboard distinguishes estimated earnings, pending settlement, payout review and completed transfer. Stripe does not automatically pay your personal bank account through this programme.

Any expected date is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival time. Read the initiative terms and save your payout destination privately in the participant dashboard.

Everyone can buy and support.

Buy through an approved participant

Use their personal link and, if offered, their issued discount code. All verified clean profit from that eligible sale goes to that participant.

Buy directly from this website

For direct eligible The Guide App sales, 50% of verified net profit is allocated for orphanages. This is a share of net profit after actual fees, confirmed applicable tax and adjustments, not 50% of the purchase price.

An allocation is not evidence of a completed donation. Confirmed transfers to selected orphanages will be recorded separately. No orphanage is named as having received funds before a transfer is confirmed.

These app-sale arrangements are separate from Amazon book purchases. This website does not claim that an Amazon purchase creates a MERCY initiative commission or orphanage allocation.
