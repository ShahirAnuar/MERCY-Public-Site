My payments

Please save the verified payment destination for your earnings. Select Bank transfer and enter your bank name, account holder’s name and account number, then save. Historical account holders can also update payment details for previously earned balances. Use your own account, or the institution’s registered account for institution payouts.

Why payments take time / Mengapa bayaran mengambil masa

The publisher’s Stripe account is currently configured for a 5-business-day settlement delay with daily payouts, subject to Stripe’s transaction and account checks. Bank posting and the publisher’s transfer processing add time. Initial payouts, verification, weekends or holidays can delay receipt. Your transaction’s actual availability date is shown when Stripe supplies it; no fixed arrival date is promised.

Dana pelanggan diselesaikan oleh Stripe kepada penerbit terlebih dahulu. Selepas dana dan pendapatan disahkan, penerbit membuat pindahan kepada anda. Tarikh dana tersedia bukan bukti wang sudah masuk ke akaun anda.

Why can't I see my earnings?

Earnings and sales are shown only after verified information is returned by MERCY's secure reports service. A missing number does not imply zero sales. Contact support if you expect a report.

Where do buyers use my coupon?

On Stripe's secure payment page, not on mercyseries.com. A valid coupon can reduce the RM25 price by RM1–RM15. Coupons must first be issued and registered to your own account.

When can I withdraw money?

Save your payout details here, then request processing. Only verified eligible earnings can be processed. The dashboard shows the confirmed schedule and payment records; requesting does not itself send money.

Can I share the link without a coupon?

Yes. Your personal sales link opens the standard RM25 Stripe checkout and can carry your affiliate reference for reconciliation.
