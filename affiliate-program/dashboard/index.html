Why can't I see my earnings?

Earnings and sales are shown only after verified information is returned by MERCY's secure reports service. A missing number does not imply zero sales. Contact support if you expect a report.

Where do buyers use my coupon?

On Stripe's secure payment page, not on mercyseries.com. A valid coupon can reduce the RM16 price by RM1–RM7. Coupons must first be issued and registered to your own account.

When can I withdraw money?

A payout schedule and bank verification are required before withdrawals can be processed. This dashboard will display verified payout status when connected.

Can I share the link without a coupon?

Yes. Your personal sales link opens the standard RM16 Stripe checkout and can carry your affiliate reference for reconciliation.
