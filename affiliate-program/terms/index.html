Initiative terms

Public affiliate enrolment is closed. Earlier KKM staff/pensioner and UiTM affiliate offers and special affiliate access have been withdrawn.

1. Who may participate

Only approved orphans, active orphanages and OKU participants may receive free app access and sell through these MERCY initiatives. The orphan/orphanage initiative is For The Ones Left Behind (Bagi Yang Ditinggalkan). The OKU initiative is For Those Who Needs a Helping Hand (Bagi Yang Memerlukan). A book or app purchase by a member of the public does not create eligibility.

A child’s application, account and payout arrangements must be managed by a parent, legal guardian or authorised adult representative as appropriate. An adult individual may apply for themselves. Orphanages apply through an authorised adult representative and must use a verified institution-named payout account.

2. Private application and free app access

No purchase, standard affiliate registration, joining fee or inventory purchase is required first. Submit accurate eligibility evidence privately with your verified Google account. An authorised reviewer decides the application before access and participant privileges activate. Requests may be rejected, reviewed, renewed or withdrawn if evidence is invalid or eligibility ends.

Approved applicants receive app access free. Their own participant copy is not a customer resale file or publishing licence. Customer sales must use the issued purchase link; do not redistribute private serials, sign-in codes or your own app copy.

3. Price, discounts and clean profit

The Guide App public list price is RM25. A participant may sell without a discount or offer an active account-owned issued coupon for RM1–RM15 off. Coupons are not stackable unless expressly supported at checkout.

For eligible attributed participant sales, the participant receives 100% of verified clean profit; publisher share is RM0. Clean profit is actual receipts less actual payment-processing fees and confirmed applicable tax, with refunds, disputes and relevant reversals accounted for. A click, application, free copy or abandoned checkout creates no earnings.

Stripe Malaysia’s standard domestic-card pricing is 3% + RM1.00, not 0.3% + RM1. For example, before applicable tax, a RM25 payment has an illustrative RM1.75 fee and RM23.25 clean profit; a RM10 payment after an RM15 discount has an illustrative RM1.30 fee and RM8.70 clean profit. Actual transaction fees, payment method, card country, currency conversion or account pricing may differ. Actual verified records determine final earnings.

4. Public direct purchases and orphanage allocation

Anyone may buy through an approved participant or directly on this website. An eligible participant sale follows the 100% clean-profit rule above. For direct eligible The Guide App sales without a beneficiary attribution, 50% of verified net profit is allocated for orphanages. Net profit is calculated after actual fees, confirmed applicable tax and adjustments.

An allocation or reserve is not a completed donation. Beneficiary selection and confirmed transfers are recorded separately; no orphanage is represented as having received funds before that transfer is confirmed. These allocations do not include unrelated Amazon retailer purchases.

5. Settlement, reconciliation and participant payouts

Stripe settles payments to MERCY’s merchant bank account according to its actual account settlement and payout schedule. The merchant account is currently configured for a five-business-day settlement delay and daily payouts. First payouts can take 7–14 days. Actual availability also depends on payment method, bank processing, weekends and public holidays. Verification or risk checks can cause additional delay.

After funds become available and fees, tax and sales are reconciled, MERCY arranges the participant’s bank transfer. This programme does not automatically send Stripe payouts directly to a participant’s bank. The dashboard shows the distinction between estimated earnings, pending settlement, payout review and completed transfers. Any expected date is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Save an accurate payout destination privately in the participant dashboard. Orphanage payouts must use the verified institution’s registered bank account. A guardian manages the appropriate payout arrangements for a child. Requested, approved, batched or pending payment is not the same as a completed transfer.

6. Historical accounts and earned balances

Withdrawal of public, KKM or UiTM affiliate privileges stops new participation and sales privileges under those earlier categories. It does not erase a genuine earlier purchase, an earned historical commission or a completed payout. Historical amounts remain subject to their applicable sale-time records and normal fee, refund, dispute and reconciliation checks.

7. Honest sharing

There are no recruitment payments, downlines, salary or fixed-income guarantees. Disclose your financial interest and describe the story and app accurately. Do not promise medical treatment, IQ gains or guaranteed genius. Amazon book sales and independent third-party referral schemes are separate; do not present them as sales in these app initiatives.

8. Privacy and support

Use the private application for proof and the participant dashboard for payout details. Do not publish eligibility documents, private serials, passwords, sign-in codes or bank details. Read the privacy notice. Contact shahir@mercyseries.com for a correction, private review or payout question.
