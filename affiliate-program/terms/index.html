Affiliate Terms

Launch notice: Online self-registration, affiliate attribution, coupon requests and commission payouts are not active until the secure systems and relevant legal checks are complete. There is no manual approval queue for ordinary eligible registrations once activated; automatic eligibility and fraud checks still apply.

1. Programme and eligibility

The MERCY Affiliate Programme is operated by MERCY Series Global for marketing the digital edition of The Guide in Malaysia. Applicants must be at least 18, sign in with a verified Google account, provide truthful contact details and accept the programme terms. Registration is online and self-service, subject to automated identity, eligibility, fraud and legal compliance checks; no routine human approval is planned. Acceptance does not create an employment relationship, guarantee income or confer publishing rights.

2. No fee or recruitment commissions

There is no joining fee, inventory purchase requirement or payment for introducing new affiliates. Commissions arise solely from eligible, independently verified purchases of the actual digital app. There is no multi-level commission, downline or recruitment bonus.

3. Price and commission formula

The digital list price is RM16. Registered affiliates may share their attributed link at full price, or later request an individual account-bound coupon offering RM1, RM2, RM3, RM4, RM5, RM6 or RM7 off. For an eligible domestic-card purchase, assuming a processing fee of exactly 3% of the discounted amount plus RM1, the affiliate commission is the paid amount minus that processing fee minus RM7, capped at RM7.52 without a coupon. The publisher's RM7 amount is after this assumed payment-processing fee only and before all other expenses or taxes. Additional payment method fees can change the actual margin and the programme must handle those cases before enabling payouts. Discount codes may not be combined unless expressly authorised. No reward is earned merely from a link click, referral registration or abandoned checkout.

4. Attribution and payment reconciliation

The checkout service must record the affiliate ID and, if used, the corresponding coupon ownership when an order is created; a payment must be verified by Stripe's server notification. Each purchase may produce only one commission. Attribution must not expose the purchaser's private activation serial or Google identity. Commissions may be held for a verification and refund period. The exact payout cadence and method will be published before affiliate sales are activated; registering does not itself earn or guarantee a commission. The affiliate can request an individually identified coupon only after successful registration, and each coupon must be associated with that same affiliate ID.

5. Refunds, reversals and misconduct

Refunded, reversed, fraudulent, disputed, self-referred or duplicate orders are not eligible for commission. Overpayments and reversed commissions may be deducted from later payouts, subject to applicable law. Attempts to impersonate MERCY, mislead customers, spoof attribution, share activation serials, abuse coupons or use bots may result in suspension and review.

6. Marketing standards and consumer information

Affiliates must disclose that they receive a commission; describe the book and its price accurately; make no false health, educational, commercial, religious or Amazon ranking guarantees; avoid spam and unsolicited commercial contact; comply with applicable marketing, privacy and consumer-protection law; and respect intellectual property. Actual discounted prices must be visible and confirmed before the customer pays. Affiliates are independent promoters, not authorised to collect cash, issue serials or make commitments on the publisher's behalf.

7. In-person sales and legal clearance

Door-to-door, in-person solicitation, direct selling and related activity may be governed by the Direct Sales and Anti-Pyramid Scheme Act 1993 (Act 500), including licensing requirements. No such activity is authorised by these terms until the business has confirmed with KPDN whether the planned activity requires licensing and obtained any required approval. This document is not evidence of a direct-sales licence.

8. Personal data and payments

Application and payout information are processed only as described in the privacy notice. No private bank account or identity document should be sent through public share links, ordinary referral messages or this preliminary application form. Tax obligations depend on individual circumstances; participants should keep commission records and seek suitable professional advice.

9. Changes and termination

We may suspend new registrations or sales for security or legal compliance checks even though standard registration is self-service. Material programme rule changes require updated published terms and appropriate notification/consent for existing participants; accrued, approved obligations will be handled fairly and according to applicable law. Either party may end participation, subject to reconciliation of eligible outstanding commissions.

10. Contact and governing law

Contact shahir@mercyseries.com for queries or disputes. These terms are intended for Malaysia and must be reviewed for applicable Malaysian law before activation. They do not exclude statutory consumer, privacy or other legal rights.

Source guidance for compliance review: Act 500, PDPA, and 2024 electronic trade regulations.
