Affiliate Terms

Online registration is open. Eligible applicants sign in with their own Google account and submit the online form. The account receives a personal affiliate identifier. Only codes confirmed as issued by Stripe are valid customer discounts.

1. Programme and eligibility

The MERCY Affiliate Programme is operated by MERCY Series Global for marketing the digital edition of The Guide in Malaysia. Applicants must be at least 18, sign in with a verified Google account, provide truthful contact details and accept the programme terms. Standard registration is online and self-service, subject to identity, eligibility, fraud and legal compliance checks. Special community benefits require separate private evidence review and owner approval. Acceptance does not create an employment relationship, guarantee income or confer publishing rights.

2. No fee or recruitment commissions

There is no joining fee, inventory purchase requirement or payment for introducing new affiliates. Commissions arise solely from eligible, independently verified purchases of the actual digital app. There is no multi-level commission, downline or recruitment bonus.

2A. Affiliate family-use app version

Every registered standard affiliate may receive one family-use copy of The Guide App, marked “Affiliate version — Not for sale”. It is designed to register under one Gmail account and, once the family licence is fully activated server-side, allow up to seven concurrent family users using that same Gmail account. Verified UiTM Faculty of Pharmacy staff remain eligible for one free personal/family copy after approval. Verified UiTM Faculty of Pharmacy students follow the RM20 student-purchase policy in section 3B instead.

Affiliate status by itself does not include an event ticket. Tickets are governed by the separate publisher-net threshold in section 3C.

Your affiliate family copy cannot be resold, transferred or gifted. It remains tied to the Gmail account registered to the affiliate. Family members may use the same affiliate family licence under that account, subject to the seven-concurrent-user family limit once the server-side family licence is fully active.

The free copy is assigned to the registered affiliate account and labelled “Affiliate version — Not for sale”. It has no resale, redistribution or publishing rights and generates no purchase commission or purchase-referral reward. Commissions remain available from eligible customer purchases through the affiliate’s own sales link. Refreshing the account or requesting delivery again does not create additional free copies.

3. Price and commission formula

The digital public list price is RM25. An affiliate may share their personal link at full price or offer an account-owned RM1–RM15 discount code. The standard affiliate commission is RM20 on an eligible RM25 sale without a coupon. Each coupon reduces the affiliate commission target by its face value. Processing fees come from the publisher’s remainder. The allocation is before either party’s other taxes or expenses and applies prospectively to eligible purchases under the updated policy.

For example, a RM25 sale with an assumed RM1.75 processing fee gives the standard affiliate RM20 and the publisher an estimated RM3.25. A RM20 sale created by a RM5 coupon gives the affiliate RM15 and leaves an estimated RM3.40 after an assumed RM1.60 fee. A RM10 sale created by a RM15 coupon gives the affiliate RM5 and leaves an estimated RM3.70 after an assumed RM1.30 fee. These examples use an assumed 3% + RM1 fee; actual processing fees, tax, refunds and disputes can change the final payable amounts. No commission is earned from a click, registration or abandoned checkout. Discounts cannot be combined unless explicitly authorised.

3A. Verified OKU and institution benefits

Approved OKU affiliates and approved active orphanages or orphan schools receive 100% of verified clean profit from eligible attributed sales made after approval, while their benefit remains valid. Clean profit means the amount actually received for that sale less the actual Stripe fee and confirmed applicable tax deductions. The publisher retains RM0 from those eligible sales. Coupons reduce the sale amount; fees, tax, refunds, disputes and anti-fraud checks can change or prevent a payout. No fixed income is guaranteed. This special rule replaces the standard publisher allocation only for an approved beneficiary’s eligible sales; it does not automatically reprice earlier sales.

Applicants must use the private community-access form, explicitly consent to necessary evidence processing and provide truthful proof. OKU applications require a readable OKU card and authorised submission by the cardholder or a parent/legal guardian. An institution’s authorised adult representative must provide registration or relevant operating authorisation and current operating evidence. Owner review is required; file scanning does not establish government authenticity. Institutions must use a bank account in the verified institution’s registered name and renew evidence when approval expires. Benefits may be suspended or revoked if proof is invalid or eligibility changes.

An approved OKU cardholder may separately receive one free personal app copy, issued to the verified applicant’s email. A guardian may manage the application and access for the cardholder. This free copy cannot be sold or gifted, generates no affiliate commission, and remains subject to the app’s serial, email sign-in and device controls. Duplicate claims are not eligible. A rejection can be corrected and resubmitted, or referred to support for review.

Daily payout review and preparation take place at 10:00 PM Malaysia time. This schedule does not guarantee bank receipt at that time. Actual Stripe fees, tax confirmation, available funds and bank processing must be reconciled before transfer.

3B. UiTM Faculty of Pharmacy access and affiliates

Active Faculty of Pharmacy staff: after private proof approval, receive the UiTM bundle FOC: The Guide App, one event ticket to The Digital Age and The Inevitable Rise of The Polymaths, and entitlement to the first batch of Morality: The Vocation of Consciousness & The AI Dilemma when released.

Active Faculty of Pharmacy students: after private proof approval, may purchase the same bundle for RM20 instead of the normal RM25 public price. The student ticket threshold is RM15 publisher net from that individual purchase. Under the current illustrative 3% + RM1 processing-fee assumption, RM20 leaves about RM18.40 publisher net and therefore qualifies. The mathematical gross price that leaves exactly RM15 under that fee assumption is about RM16.50.

After a qualifying student purchase, the student is also offered the affiliate opportunity. Affiliate status alone does not create an additional event ticket.

Approved active Faculty of Pharmacy UiTM affiliates may continue to receive any separately approved affiliate benefit from eligible attributed sales while approval remains valid. Coupons, payment verification, tax confirmation, refunds, disputes and anti-fraud checks continue to apply.

This is an independent MERCY programme for eligible participants. It is not an official UiTM programme or university endorsement.

3C. Direct-purchase bundle and event-ticket threshold

A direct, non-affiliate, non-coupon RM25 purchase includes The Guide App immediately and entitlement to the first batch of Morality: The Vocation of Consciousness & The AI Dilemma when released. One event ticket is also included when the individual purchase leaves at least RM20 publisher net after payment processing and any applicable transaction deductions. Under the current illustrative 3% + RM1 fee assumption, RM25 leaves about RM23.25 and therefore qualifies. The gross price that would produce exactly RM20 before any other deductions under that same assumption is approximately RM21.65.

Affiliate status alone never grants a ticket. Under the standard RM20 affiliate-commission policy, an RM25 affiliate sale leaves only about RM3.25 publisher remainder after the assumed RM1.75 fee, so it does not qualify. The RM20 UiTM student purchase leaves about RM18.40 after the assumed RM1.60 fee, so it also does not automatically qualify for a ticket.

4. Attribution and payment reconciliation

The checkout service must record the affiliate ID and, if used, the corresponding coupon ownership when an order is created; a payment must be verified by Stripe's server notification. Each purchase may produce only one commission. Attribution must not expose the purchaser's private activation serial or Google identity. Commissions may be held for a verification and refund period. Affiliates may save DuitNow or bank payout details in their authenticated dashboard and request processing. A request does not itself transfer funds. The dashboard shows any confirmed processing schedule; completion depends on verified eligible earnings, available funds and bank processing. Registering does not itself earn or guarantee commission. The affiliate can request an individually identified coupon only after successful registration, and each coupon must be associated with that same affiliate ID.

5. Refunds, reversals and misconduct

Refunded, reversed, fraudulent, disputed, self-referred or duplicate orders are not eligible for commission. Overpayments and reversed commissions may be deducted from later payouts, subject to applicable law. Attempts to impersonate MERCY, mislead customers, spoof attribution, share activation serials, abuse coupons or use bots may result in suspension and review.

6. Marketing standards and consumer information

Affiliates must disclose that they receive a commission; describe the book and its price accurately; make no false health, educational, commercial, religious or Amazon ranking guarantees; avoid spam and unsolicited commercial contact; comply with applicable marketing, privacy and consumer-protection law; and respect intellectual property. Actual discounted prices must be visible and confirmed before the customer pays. Affiliates are independent promoters, not authorised to collect cash, issue serials or make commitments on the publisher's behalf.

7. In-person sales and legal clearance

Door-to-door, in-person solicitation, direct selling and related activity may be governed by the Direct Sales and Anti-Pyramid Scheme Act 1993 (Act 500), including licensing requirements. No such activity is authorised by these terms until the business has confirmed with KPDN whether the planned activity requires licensing and obtained any required approval. This document is not evidence of a direct-sales licence.

8. Personal data and payments

Application and payout information are processed only as described in the privacy notice. No private bank account or identity document should be sent through public share links, ordinary referral messages or the standard registration form. Special-benefit evidence must use the authenticated private community-access form. Payout information must be submitted through the authenticated payout form. Tax obligations depend on individual circumstances; participants should keep commission records and seek suitable professional advice.

9. Changes and termination

We may suspend new registrations or sales for security or legal compliance checks even though standard registration is self-service. Material programme rule changes require updated published terms and appropriate notification/consent for existing participants; accrued, approved obligations will be handled fairly and according to applicable law. Either party may end participation, subject to reconciliation of eligible outstanding commissions.
