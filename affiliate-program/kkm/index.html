Upload proof. Keep it private.

Upload proof to apply for the KKM staff benefit. Your application is privately reviewed; uploading a file does not automatically approve eligibility.

Use one or two JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF files, up to 3 MB each. Keep only the information needed for eligibility review visible. Cover unrelated private details. Do not include passwords, sign-in codes or bank information.

Documents are encrypted and available only to authorised reviewers. Proof access expires after 30 days; daily cleanup removes expired files. Approval includes a review expiry, and fresh proof may be required to renew it.

What does 100% clean profit mean?

The amount actually received for an eligible sale, minus actual Stripe fees and confirmed applicable tax deductions. The remaining amount belongs to the approved affiliate; the publisher receives RM0. Coupons, refunds, disputes and failed payments can change the final amount.

ExampleNo couponRM7 coupon
Customer paysRM16.00RM9.00
Assumed Stripe feeRM1.48RM1.27
Provisional affiliate share, before taxRM14.52RM7.73
Publisher shareRM0.00RM0.00

Examples use an assumed 3% + RM1 fee. Actual fees and confirmed applicable tax determine settled earnings. No fixed earnings are promised.

Payout review and preparation take place daily at 10:00 PM Malaysia time. Transfer completion depends on verified fees and tax, available funds and bank processing. Bank receipt at 10:00 PM is not guaranteed.

Before you begin

Does registration immediately activate the 100% rate?

No. Registration creates the standard affiliate account and free personal app access. The KKM rate starts after private eligibility approval and applies to eligible later sales while the approval remains valid.

Who can apply for the KKM benefit?

This benefit is for active staff of Kementerian Kesihatan Malaysia. Upload proof for private review. Eligibility is confirmed before the special rate begins.

What if I have a problem?

Use the private support textbox and track our reply. Send eligibility proof only through the private application form, not in support messages.
