OKU participants participate under For Those Who Needs a Helping Hand (Bagi Yang Memerlukan). Participation requires private owner verification; this page does not approve or activate anyone.

Sales, discounts and actual net profit

Approved participants may offer discounts up to RM15 and keep 100% of actual net profit from their eligible attributed app sales after actual payment fees, confirmed applicable tax, refunds and disputes. The public may buy through an approved participant or directly from the website. Existing valid participant identifiers and coupon attribution must be retained.

For direct website app sales, 50% of actual net profit is allocated to an orphanage fund. An allocation or reserve is not a completed donation. No completed donation is claimed without a verified transfer record. Amazon book orders are separate from website app sales and do not earn this programme’s app commission.

Fees and payment timing

The standard processing-fee estimate is 3% + RM1; actual Stripe fees control. For example, RM25 paid less an estimated RM1.75 fee leaves RM23.25 before any confirmed applicable tax, refunds or disputes. This is an illustration, not a verified settlement or payout.

Stripe currently settles this Malaysian merchant account daily after a configured 5-business-day delay. Bank posting and the publisher’s later participant transfer can add time. Stripe does not automatically pay participants directly. Pending, available and paid amounts are distinct.
