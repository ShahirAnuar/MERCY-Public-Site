Personal Data Protection Notice / Notis Perlindungan Data Peribadi

Collection is currently disabled. The application form does not send or store personal information until the secure application service confirms it is enabled. This notice explains the intended processing; final wording must be reviewed before the service opens.
Pengumpulan data masih ditutup. Borang tidak menghantar atau menyimpan maklumat peribadi sehingga perkhidmatan pendaftaran selamat diaktifkan.

1. Data controller / Pengawal data

MERCY Series Global (site-published SSM registration 202603256493 (TR0350191-X)). Privacy contact: shahir@mercyseries.com. The registered address and any mandatory privacy contacts will be checked and published before applications open.

2. Data collected / Data dikumpulkan

To review applications: full name, email, contact/WhatsApp number, country/region, proposed marketing channel, an optional audience description, age-18 declaration, terms/privacy acknowledgement and optional separate marketing consent. Technical abuse-prevention logs (for example request timestamps or IP metadata) may be necessary for security. No bank account, IC/passport number, health data or payment card details are requested on this page. Payout details will be requested separately only when necessary through a secure authenticated channel.

Untuk menilai permohonan: nama penuh, e-mel, nombor telefon, lokasi, saluran promosi, penerangan pilihan, pengesahan umur dan persetujuan berkenaan terma/notis. Maklumat akaun bank dan dokumen pengenalan tidak diminta melalui borang ini.

3. Purpose / Tujuan

We intend to use the data to assess eligibility, communicate application status, prevent fraud, issue approved affiliate identifiers, attribute eligible purchases, administer commissions and payouts, and comply with law. Marketing messages require a separate optional choice. We will not publish applicants' private details or send activation serials to affiliates.

Data bertujuan untuk penilaian kelayakan, komunikasi keputusan, keselamatan, pengurusan pautan dan kupon, rekod komisen dan kewajipan undang-undang. Mesej pemasaran memerlukan pilihan persetujuan berasingan.

4. Disclosure and service providers / Perkongsian

Access is restricted to authorised programme administrators and service providers necessary for secure hosting, communications, fraud checking, payments and recordkeeping. Where personal data crosses borders, appropriate legal safeguards and transparency will be required. Data is not publicly published and is not sold to advertisers.

5. Retention and security / Tempoh simpanan dan keselamatan

We will retain application and financial records only for a documented purpose and legal retention period; different retention periods may apply to rejected applications versus completed payments. Security controls must include encrypted transport, limited access, audit trails, safe deletion and incident response. Data retention rules will be finalised before registration opens.

6. Your rights / Hak anda

You may contact shahir@mercyseries.com to request access to, correction of, or other legally available controls over your personal data, or to withdraw optional marketing consent. Some financial records may need to be kept for legal compliance.

Anda boleh menghubungi alamat e-mel di atas untuk memohon akses, pembetulan atau hak lain yang dibenarkan oleh undang-undang, termasuk menarik balik persetujuan pemasaran pilihan. Sesetengah rekod kewangan tertakluk pada kewajipan simpanan undang-undang.

7. Questions / Pertanyaan

Read about your rights under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709) and amendments. Contact us at shahir@mercyseries.com for programme privacy questions.
