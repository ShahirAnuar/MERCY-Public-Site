What does “clean profit” mean? The amount actually received for an eligible sale, less the actual Stripe processing fee and confirmed applicable tax deductions. The remaining amount goes to the approved beneficiary; the publisher’s share is RM0. It is not 100% of the customer’s purchase price. Coupons reduce the sale amount, and refunds or disputes can reduce or reverse earnings. No fixed income is promised.

Payout review and preparation take place daily at 10:00 PM Malaysia time. Transfer completion depends on verified fees and tax, available funds and bank processing; this is not a guarantee that money reaches the bank at 10:00 PM.

Private proof review

Show eligibility with care.

For OKU: scan or upload one clear OKU card. Keep the cardholder’s name and JKM issuer visible. You may cover unrelated MyKad details, address, diagnosis and barcode. Do not upload medical reports or a selfie.

For an orphanage or orphan school: upload two documents: registration or relevant operating authorisation, and evidence dated within the past 180 days that the institution is currently operating. Provide its registered name, registration number and official website or registry link.

Current care-centre, school or relevant religious-authority documentation may be used. Organisation registration alone does not establish that an orphanage or school is active. Do not send children’s photographs, student lists or residents’ personal data.

Accepted files: JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 3 MB each. Reviewers may request a clearer copy or an official status confirmation if the evidence is insufficient.

What the website checks

The system checks file type and size, account access and duplicate submissions. An authorised owner then reviews the proof before approving a benefit.

JKM’s public OKU status lookup is no longer available. A scan cannot establish authenticity by itself. We do not ask for MyGOV login details or claim an automatic government identity check.

Your proof stays private. Documents are encrypted and available only to authorised reviewers. Proof access expires after 30 days; daily cleanup removes expired files. They are not published or sent to the AI chatbot.

A few practical questions

Can I use both OKU benefits?

Eligible cardholders can apply separately for personal app access and the affiliate benefit. Affiliate earnings require a registered account, approval and genuine eligible paid sales. A free app claim does not generate a commission.

How does a free copy reach me?

After approval, MERCY sends the app link and a private serial to the verified applicant’s email. Enter the serial in the app, request a fresh email sign-in code, then follow the installation steps. A guardian manages access for the person they represent.

What if the application is rejected?

Check the reason in My applications. You may correct the information and submit clearer evidence, or contact support for a review. Do not post your card or proof in a public message.

Which bank account can an orphanage use?

The affiliate account must be managed by an authorised adult representative. Approved institutions must use a bank account in the verified institution’s registered name. Personal DuitNow or personal bank destinations are not accepted for institution payouts.

Does approval last forever?

Personal free access is issued once per approved cardholder. Affiliate benefits can be reviewed, suspended or revoked if evidence is invalid or eligibility changes. Institutions must renew their evidence by the expiry shown in their approval. Existing payout checks for fees, tax, refunds and disputes continue to apply.
