For The Ones Left Behind

Bagi Yang Ditinggalkan

Free app access and 100% of clean profit on eligible participant sales for approved orphans and active orphanages. A responsible adult or guardian manages a child’s application and payment arrangements.

For Those In Need of a Helping Hand

Bagi Yang Memerlukan

Free app access and 100% of clean profit on eligible participant sales for approved OKU participants. Apply yourself, or through a parent or legal guardian where appropriate.

Proof stays private.

OKU: one readable OKU card. Keep the cardholder’s name and JKM issuer visible. Cover unrelated MyKad details, address, diagnosis and barcode. Do not upload medical reports or a selfie.

Orphan: one redacted official document or institution confirmation establishing eligibility and the applicant’s authority where relevant. A parent or legal guardian can submit on behalf of a child. Do not send children’s photographs, a family’s full identity documents or unnecessary sensitive details.

Orphanage: registration or relevant operating authorisation, plus distinct current operations evidence dated within the last 180 days. Provide the institution’s registered name and registration number. An official website or registry link is optional.

JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF only, up to 3 MB per file. Evidence is encrypted and visible to authorised reviewers. Proof access expires after 30 days; daily cleanup removes expired files. Limited decisions and consent records may remain to administer access and financial records.
