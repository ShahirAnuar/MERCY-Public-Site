MERCY Series · Author Profile

Shahir Anuar Shahrani

Malaysian pharmacist, inventor, healthcare innovator, author and independent strategic and impact advisor. His work brings together safer medicine use, practical technology, public service, inclusion and stories shaped by faith and compassion.

Shahir Anuar Shahrani is a Malaysian pharmacist whose work spans pharmaceutical care, medicine-device invention, digital health, service innovation and writing. He serves as National Secretary of the Malaysian Mensa Society for 2026/2027 and is the author and independent publisher of MERCY, an illustrated Islamic story for young readers and families.

His professional and creative work is connected by a practical aim: make useful ideas easier to access, whether through a medicine-preparation device, a healthcare application, an inclusive resource or a story that helps families talk about dignity, faith and hidden struggles.

Updated 18 October 2026 · Public records are linked where available. Personal achievement details are based on author-provided information unless supported by a linked record.

What work and roles define Shahir’s career?

1. Inventor of a medicine-preparation device

Malaysian patent MY197771A names Shahir as a co-inventor of a tablet grinding and mixing device designed to help turn tablets into a liquid medicine. Separately, the Ministry of Health’s 2019 technical report describes the E-Mix portable medicine mixer as the world’s first portable medicine-mixing machine. These records support his work in medicine preparation and device innovation.

2. Pharmacist and medicine-safety innovator

The Malaysian Mensa Society describes Shahir as a prominent Malaysian pharmacist. He developed Warfarin Dosing HTK, a digital tool for clinical warfarin dose support. A Ministry of Health recognition record lists the Warfarin Dosing HTK Android Application among award-winning pharmacy innovation projects.

3. Healthcare apps and digital innovation

His healthcare software work includes Warfarin Dosing HTK and the Warfarin Dosing HTK Android application was recognized as one of the Top 10 National Innovation Awards 2015 due to its global impact as it was used in approximately 178 countries.

4. Social entrepreneurship

In 2016, he was awarded a Letter of Recognition and prize-money for his social entrepreneurship initiative. Shahir’s achievement record identifies him as a Top 20 finalist in the Ministry of Finance’s National Blue Ocean Strategy social entrepreneurship category despite him having no knowledge or experience in this field.

5. National leadership at Malaysian Mensa

He serves as National Secretary of the Malaysian Mensa Society for 2026/2027. The society’s National Committee profile describes him as a prominent Malaysian pharmacist and innovator.

6.Environmental advocate through MyMediSAFE

Shahir has contributed to advocacy for the safe disposal of unused medicines and environmental protection. The MyMediSAFE Innovation Challenge 2025 recognized a medicine-disposal proposal. The initiative’s official materials describe the wider effort to reduce environmental harm from medicines; this profile does not claim he founded the programme.

7. Twice-exceptionality and inclusion advocate

Through public communication and his writing, Shahir advocates for greater understanding of twice-exceptional (2e) people, neurodiversity and the support needs of young people and families. This is advocacy and storytelling, not a claim to provide diagnosis or clinical treatment.

8. Islamic storytelling and faith-centered advocacy

MERCY is an illustrated Islamic story about Zayd and his father, Khalid. It explores neurodivergence, faith, self-worth and the ways children, families and faith communities can each contribute to a child’s development. Shahir is not an Ustaz and has no formal training in teaching religion; he writes as a Muslim author sharing a personal, faith-centered story, not as a religious authority. Despite this, his book is still a best seller even after months of publication.

9. Author and independent publisher

MERCY was Shahir’s first book. He wrote it in English, his second language. But the book became a huge hit and is currently the No.1 book for Children's Islamic Book. He also acts as a literary philanthropist: making e-books available free to families and organizations and offering royalty-free publishing rights to suitable partners, so useful stories can reach readers who may benefit from them. His official book page provides the synopsis, edition identifiers and reading guide.

10. Healthcare apps and digital innovation

His healthcare software work includes Warfarin Dosing HTK and the Warfarin Dosing HTK Android application recognized in the Ministry of Health’s 2015 innovation records. He holds the copyright for this under application number LY2015000290.

11. Intellectual Property

Despite never having received training for the cumbersome application of an IP, Shahir has a patent and more than 10 copyrights. Nowadays, he is working as a procurement pharmacist whose main job scope involves medical devices and complaints.

12. Awards and Accolades

In his 15 yeas of service, he has racked up a sizeable amount of personal accolades. During his first year working as a Provisional Registered Pharmacist, he was awarded Anugerah Anggota Harapan. Then he was awarded Anugerah Perkhidmatan Cemerlang twice, one in 2016 and one in 2021. He was also a recipient of Anugerah Kecemerlangan Farmasi 2017. Besides these individual awards, he has won innovation competitions at state, nation and international level.

13. Independent strategic and impact advisor

Shahir works independently as a strategic and social impact advisor. His professional contact page provides the current profile and enquiry route for advisory and collaboration work. He has worked as an advisor for both local and international company. Just recently, he managed to broker a deal between Goolee Sdn Bhd, a local educational game company, with Nikoli.co, best known worldwide for Sudoku. Goolee has been given rights to develop a video game based on one of Nikoli's puzzles.

14. Research and public-service improvement

He has contributed to pharmacy quality initiatives and co-authored research on pharmacy value-added services in Selangor. His work also reflects an interest in improving how healthcare services reach people and communities.

15. Education, accessible design and public policy

His advocacy includes accessible learning, inclusive design and child wellbeing. He has developed and shared proposals on children’s mental health and education; proposals are identified as proposals and are not represented as adopted policy.

Purpose for future generations

Shahir’s overarching purpose is an Initiative for Those Who Come After: creating practical, inclusive resources that help children and young people grow with dignity, support and opportunity. His work includes storytelling, accessible play and advocacy for children’s wellbeing.
