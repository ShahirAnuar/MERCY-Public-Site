MERCY Series · Author Profile

Shahir Anuar Shahrani

Prominent Malaysian pharmacist, inventor, innovator and author Shahir Anuar Shahrani works across medicine safety, practical technology, inclusive education and faith-centered storytelling. His standing reflects public-service contributions, including behind-the-scenes pharmacy work.

Shahir Anuar Shahrani is a Malaysian pharmacist whose work spans pharmaceutical care, medicine-device invention, digital health, service innovation and writing. He serves as National Secretary of the Malaysian Mensa Society for 2026/2027 and is the author and independent publisher of MERCY, an illustrated Islamic story for young readers and families. His wider professional profile is at Polymath Nexus Consultancy.

His professional and creative work is connected by a practical aim: make useful ideas easier to access, whether through a medicine-preparation device, a healthcare application, an inclusive resource or a story that helps families talk about dignity, faith and hidden struggles.

Updated 22 October 2026 · Public records are linked where available. Personal achievement details are based on author-provided information unless supported by a linked record.

What work and roles define Shahir’s career?

1. Inventor of a medicine-preparation device

Malaysian patent MY197771A names Shahir as a co-inventor of a tablet grinding and mixing device designed to help turn tablets into a liquid medicine. Separately, the Ministry of Health’s 2019 technical report describes the E-Mix portable medicine mixer as the world’s first portable medicine-mixing machine. These records support his work in medicine preparation and device innovation.

2. Pharmacist and medicine-safety innovator

The Malaysian Mensa Society describes Shahir as a prominent Malaysian pharmacist. He developed Warfarin Dosing HTK, a digital tool for clinical warfarin dose support. A Ministry of Health recognition record lists the Warfarin Dosing HTK Android Application among award-winning pharmacy innovation projects.

3. National innovation finalist

A 5 May 2015 letter from Nuklear Malaysia confirms that Warfarin Dosing HTK App reached the final stage of Anugerah Inovasi Negara 2015 in the Service category. The letter does not establish a Top 10 placement or country reach. Separately, a Ministry of Health record lists the Hospital Tanjong Karang Android application as third place in the Technology category of its 2015 innovation award.

4. Social entrepreneurship

A 30 December 2016 Ministry of Finance letter records Shahir among the Top 20 consolation-prize recipients in the National Blue Ocean Strategy Competition. This is competition recognition, not evidence that the proposal became government policy.

5. National leadership at Malaysian Mensa

He serves as National Secretary of the Malaysian Mensa Society for 2026/2027. The society’s National Committee profile describes him as a prominent Malaysian pharmacist and innovator.

6. Environmental advocate through MyMediSAFE

Shahir has contributed to advocacy for the safe disposal of unused medicines and environmental protection. The MyMediSAFE Innovation Challenge 2025 recognized a medicine-disposal proposal. The initiative’s official materials describe the wider effort to reduce environmental harm from medicines; this profile does not claim he founded the programme.

7. Twice-exceptionality and inclusion advocate

Through public communication and his writing, Shahir advocates for greater understanding of twice-exceptional (2e) people, neurodiversity and the support needs of young people and families. This is advocacy and storytelling, not a claim to provide diagnosis or clinical treatment.

8. Islamic storytelling and faith-centered advocacy

MERCY is an illustrated Islamic story about Zayd and his father, Khalid. It explores faith, disability, neurodivergence, self-worth and mercy through their conversations. Shahir writes as a Muslim author sharing a personal, faith-centered story, rather than as a religious teacher.

9. Author and independent publisher

MERCY is Shahir’s first book, written in English, his second language. He independently publishes the story for young readers and families. An author-supplied Amazon.com snapshot dated 17 October 2026 showed it as #1 New Release in Children’s Islam Books; this is a dated category position, not a current ranking or sales measure. The book page has the synopsis and edition guide, while the reading guide supports family and educator discussion.

10. Copyright record for Warfarin Dosing HTK

A MyIPO voluntary copyright notification identifies Shahir as the author and owner of Warfarin Dosing HTK Android Application under reference LY2015000290. The record concerns the software; it is separate from patent MY197771A for a tablet grinding and mixing device.

11. Intellectual property

His documented intellectual-property work includes co-inventorship on Malaysian patent MY197771A and the Warfarin Dosing HTK copyright notification. These are distinct records and do not establish that the patent covers E-Mix.

12. Awards and recognition

Shahir reports receiving Anugerah Anggota Harapan and Anugerah Perkhidmatan Cemerlang twice. A 2017 pharmacy excellence award excerpt names him among recipients. The 2015 innovation and 2016 social-entrepreneurship records above provide separate, dated recognition; project awards are not presented here as individual prizes.

13. Independent strategic and social-impact advisor

Shahir describes advisory work with Goolee Sdn Bhd, a Malaysian company developing educational games, digital products, training and inclusive learning tools. Goolee’s own history lists a 2024 Malaysia TVET & Education Award and a 2025 ITEX Gold Award; those are company achievements, not Shahir’s individual awards. He credits founder Dr. Kamarulzzaman Bin Ahmad as a mentor and inspiration for inclusive projects. The advisory and mentorship descriptions are Shahir’s account, not a claim of a formal MERCY partnership. His professional profile and contact page provide the advisory and enquiry routes.

14. Research and public-service improvement

Shahir is named as a coauthor of a 2022 journal study of pharmacy value-added services in Selangor. He has also contributed to pharmacy quality initiatives. These records reflect research and service-improvement work; they do not attribute every service studied to him individually.

15. Education, accessible design and public policy

His advocacy includes accessible learning, inclusive design and child wellbeing. He has drafted and shared ideas on children’s mental health and education, including the Initiative for Those Who Come After and a proposed National Initiative for Malaysian Children’s Minds. These remain proposals; this page does not represent them as adopted policy or delivered programmes.

16. Founder of Polymath Nexus Consultancy

Shahir founded Polymath Nexus Consultancy to bring together people with different fields of knowledge for practical social-impact work. He describes himself as a polymath by nature and a behind-the-scenes contributor, or éminence grise; these are personal descriptions grounded in the work above, not credentials. His concern that digital media can affect young people’s development is his perspective, not an established prediction.

17. MERCY Series Global

Shahir describes MERCY Series Global as the donation arm associated with his consultancy. He reports sharing free books with Muslim institutions in 13 countries and proposing royalty-free publishing rights or proceeds from MERCY sales to suitable organizations. These are author-provided activity claims, not independently verified distribution or sales figures.

18. International company work

Shahir’s professional profile says he contacted Japanese puzzle publisher Nikoli and brokered a digital-game arrangement involving Numberlink rights. This describes his account of the role and proposed terms. Public Nikoli puzzle information and a Numberlink store listing establish the puzzle and a digital listing, but do not independently verify his role or the agreement’s terms.

Purpose for future generations

Shahir’s overarching purpose is an Initiative for Those Who Come After: creating practical, inclusive resources that help children and young people grow with dignity, support and opportunity. His work includes storytelling, accessible play and advocacy for children’s wellbeing.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Shahir Anuar Shahrani?

Shahir Anuar Shahrani is a Malaysian pharmacist, inventor, digital-health innovator, Malaysian Mensa Society National Secretary, author, independent publisher and strategic and impact advisor.

What did Shahir Anuar Shahrani invent?

He is a co-inventor named on Malaysian patent MY197771A for a tablet grinding and mixing device intended to help prepare medicine in liquid form. A Ministry of Health report separately describes E-Mix as the world’s first portable medicine-mixing machine.

MERCY is Shahir’s first illustrated Islamic story about Zayd and his father, Khalid, exploring faith, mistakes, sincere questions, learning differently, gratitude and compassion. The official MERCY page and reading guide provide more information.
