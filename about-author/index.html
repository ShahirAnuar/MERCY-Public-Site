MERCY Series · Author Profile

Shahir Anuar Shahrani

Prominent Malaysian pharmacist, inventor, healthcare innovator, author and independent strategic and impact advisor. His work brings together safer medicine use, practical technology, public service, inclusion and stories shaped by faith and compassion.

Shahir Anuar Shahrani is a prominent Malaysian pharmacist whose work spans pharmaceutical care, medicine-device invention, digital health, service innovation and writing. He serves as National Secretary of the Malaysian Mensa Society for 2026/2027 and is the author and independent publisher of MERCY, an illustrated Islamic story for young readers and families.

His professional and creative work is connected by a practical aim: make useful ideas easier to access, whether through a medicine-preparation device, a healthcare application, an inclusive resource or a story that helps families talk about dignity, faith and hidden struggles.

What work and roles define Shahir’s career?

Pharmacist and medicine-safety innovator

The Malaysian Mensa Society describes Shahir as a prominent Malaysian pharmacist. He developed his first app, Warfarin Dosing HTK, a digital tool mainly used for clinical warfarin dose support. The innovative app was selected as a Finalist for Anugerah Inovasi Negara (AIN) 2015 under the Ministry of Science and Technology (MOSTI) and Agency Nuclear Malaysia due to its global impact. The Ministry of Health recognition record lists the Warfarin Dosing HTK Android Application among award-winning pharmacy innovation projects. Google Play Console view records the app across 153 countries; this is a country/region acquisition measure.

Inventor of a medicine-preparation device

Malaysian patent MY197771A names Shahir as a co-inventor of a tablet grinding and mixing device designed to help turn tablets into a liquid medicine. Separately, the Ministry of Health’s 2019 technical report describes the E-Mix portable medicine mixer as the world’s first portable medicine-mixing machine. These records support his work in medicine preparation and device innovation.

Healthcare apps and digital innovation

His healthcare software work includes Warfarin Dosing HTK, an Android application appearing in the Ministry of Health’s 2015 innovation records. An official MyIPO IP Online report records the copyright under LY2015000290 (Notification No. CRLY00002404). The application was submitted on 4 March 2015 and registered/protected on 11 August 2015. The report names Shahir Anuar Bin Shahrani as both the author and copyright owner. A related statutory declaration refers to the work as “Warfarin Dosing”, while the project exhibit uses the fuller title “Warfarin Dosing HTK Android Application”.

Social entrepreneurship

In 2016, he received a Letter of Recognition and prize from the Ministry of Finance for his Social Entrepreneurship initiative that was selected as one of the Top 20 projects for the National Blue Ocean Strategy (NBOS) nationwide competition.

Public-service recognition

He has won personal accolades in his capacity of serving the country as a government pharmacist, where he has twice been awarded Anugerah Pekhidmatan Cemerlang KKM in 2016 and 2022. He has also been awarded Anugerah Kecemerlangan Farmasi KKM in 2017 . He has accumuluted a number of personal accolades since entering government service, such as Anugerah PRP Harapan 2012 (Hospital Tanjong Karang) and Anugerah Inovasi Jabatan Farmasi Hopital Tengku Ampuan Rahimah, Klang (2025). He has also won numerous national and international innovation awards

National leadership at Malaysian Mensa

He serves as National Secretary of the Malaysian Mensa Society for 2026/2027. The society’s National Committee profile describes him as a prominent Malaysian pharmacist and innovator.

Environmental advocate through MyMediSAFE

Shahir has contributed to advocacy for the safe disposal of unused medicines and environmental protection. The MyMediSAFE Innovation Challenge 2025 recognized a medicine-disposal proposal. The initiative’s official materials describe the wider effort to reduce environmental harm from medicines.

Twice-exceptionality and inclusion advocate

Through public communication and his writing, Shahir advocates for greater understanding of twice-exceptional (2e) people, neurodiversity and the support needs of young people and families. This is advocacy and storytelling, not a claim to provide diagnosis or clinical treatment.

Islamic storytelling and faith-centered advocacy

MERCY is an illustrated Islamic story about Zayd and his father, Khalid. Its themes include sincere questions, mistakes, repentance, gratitude, learning differently and compassion. Shahir writes from a faith-centered perspective as an author and advocate, not as a religious authority.

Author and independent publisher

Shahir writes and independently publishes MERCY under his own name. The book invites families and educators to discuss self-worth, faith, learning differences and kindness. His official book page provides the synopsis, edition identifiers and reading guide. It is not available for sale in Malaysia yet, but there are major book retailers that interested readers can buy from including Amazon, Walmart, Booktopia, etc...

Independent strategic and impact advisor

Shahir works independently as a strategic and social impact advisor. His professional contact page provides the current profile and enquiry route for advisory and collaboration work.

Goolee liaison and inclusive game collaboration

Shahir’s supplied professional record describes strategic liaison and intermediary work for national and international opportunities connected with Goolee Sdn Bhd, including collaborative development of educational and inclusive games. Goolee’s own materials document digital game development, accessible learning and B-Goolee’s Braille-supported work.

Nikoli and international puzzle-sector connections

Shahir’s supplied record also identifies work connecting Goolee-related opportunities with national and international stakeholders, including Nikoli Co., Ltd. Nikoli’s official materials establish its history as a Japanese puzzle publisher and its role in naming and popularising Sudoku.

Research and public-service improvement

He has contributed to Ministry of Health Pharmacy Quality Initiatives reporting guidance and pharmacy quality initiatives and co-authored record research on pharmacy value-added services in Selangor. His work also reflects an interest in improving how healthcare services reach people and communities.

Education, accessible design and public policy

His advocacy includes accessible learning, inclusive design and child wellbeing. He has developed and shared proposals on children’s mental health and education. He has shared his initiatives regarding this issue both locally, during Libat Urus Belanjawan 2027, and also with other countries, mainly major Islam organizations.

Who is Shahir Anuar Shahrani?
