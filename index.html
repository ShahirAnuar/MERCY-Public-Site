About MERCY

A father and son learning from one another

✦

MERCY is a heartfelt illustrated story about faith, family, questions, struggle and compassion. It was created for young readers and families who may see parts of themselves in a child who experiences the world differently.

Through the guidance of his father, Khalid, young Zayd discovers that falling is not failure. True balance is found in rising again with repentance, hope and faith — and in remembering that our gifts, effort and strength to continue are all acts of mercy.

Everything that we have is an act of mercy from our Lord. So be merciful to others, and be merciful to ourselves.
