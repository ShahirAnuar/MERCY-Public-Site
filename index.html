About MERCY

What is MERCY about?

✦

MERCY by Shahir Anuar Shahrani is an illustrated Islamic story for young readers, families and educators. It follows Zayd and his father, Khalid, through mistakes, sincere questions, learning differently, school pressure, illness, achievement and the possibility of beginning again. The book connects faith, repentance, gratitude, self-worth, humility and compassion, reminding readers that a person's value is not measured by perfection or approval.

Through the guidance of his father, Khalid, young Zayd discovers that falling is not failure. True balance is found in rising again with repentance, hope and faith — and in remembering that our gifts, effort and strength to continue are all acts of mercy.

Reader questions

Questions families often ask about MERCY

✦

What is MERCY about?

MERCY follows Zayd and his father Khalid through mistakes, sincere questions, learning differently, illness, school pressure and achievement. Its Islamic themes include repentance, gratitude, humility, self-worth and compassion.

Who is MERCY for?

It is intended for young readers, families and educators who want an illustrated story that can open conversations about faith, dignity, hidden struggles and kindness.

Where can I find the editions?

The official summary and edition guide explains the digital and paperback identifiers. The family and classroom reading guide offers optional discussion questions.

Is the reading guide medical advice?

No. MERCY is storytelling, and the guide is a discussion resource. Questions about a real person's treatment belong with that person's healthcare professional.
